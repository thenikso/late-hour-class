# Art in this folder

The office tileset (`tiles/office.png`) and the avatar sheets (`avatars/ava_*.png`) come from **The Agency**, a small multiplayer office game (git author: mrosati). Its `ASSET_LICENSES.md` dedicates them to the public domain under **CC0 1.0**, so they can be reused and changed freely. The credit is a courtesy.

- `tiles/office.png`: 8 × 9 tiles of 16 × 16 px.
- `avatars/ava_N.png`: 4 columns × 10 rows of 16 × 24 px frames: idle down/up/left/right, walk down/up/left/right, sit (from behind), drink.
